About Marwadi University

Marwadi University, Rajkot, Gujarat, is a leading multidisciplinary university committed to academic excellence, innovation, research, entrepreneurship, and industry-oriented education. The university provides world-class infrastructure, experienced faculty, modern laboratories, and excellent placement opportunities to prepare students for successful careers.

Vision

To become a globally recognized university by providing quality education, fostering innovation, research, leadership, and ethical values for creating competent professionals.

Mission

  • Provide quality education through innovative teaching.
  • Promote research and entrepreneurship.
  • Develop leadership and ethical values.
  • Strengthen industry-academia collaboration.
  • Encourage lifelong learning and global competence.

Program Specific Outcomes

PSO 1

Apply engineering and technical knowledge to solve real-world problems.

PSO 2

Develop innovative software and technology-based solutions using modern tools.

PSO 3

Demonstrate teamwork, leadership, communication skills, and professional ethics.
